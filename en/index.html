01 — THE LOTUS SPIRIT

A lotus doesn’t choose where it grows.
But it always chooses to rise.

Lotus is named after the flower closest to Vietnamese hearts: rooted in mud, rising through water, blooming in the sun. We believe every young person can do the same — wherever you begin, you can grow through a career you truly love.

We focus on two fields devoted to caring for people — healthcare and beauty — across Singapore, the USA, Canada and Europe. Every pathway starts from your real abilities, hopes and circumstances, never from a one-size-fits-all template.

SINGAPORE — YOUR GLOBAL LAUNCHPAD

Study in Singapore.
Think beyond it.

Singapore offers English-language study to international standards, only about two hours’ flight from Vietnam. The qualifications and hands-on skills you build here can become your launchpad towards professional roles in the USA, Canada and Europe.

And you won’t walk alone. Lotus helps you explore long-term career options and the possibility of bringing your family — based on suitable programmes and each member’s real eligibility.

QUESTIONS YOU MAY HAVE

Where should I start if I have no experience?

Start by noting your education, language skills and goals. These provide a basis for discussing suitable study or skills-development options.

Should I choose Singapore, the USA, Canada or Europe?

Your choice should reflect your preferred field, budget, language skills and long-term plans. For Europe, identify a specific country before evaluating programmes.

What should I prepare before a consultation?

Prepare your qualifications, study or work experience, language level, estimated budget and questions. You can fill in the consultation form above so our advisers can prepare.
